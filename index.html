The governance layer for delegated operational execution

Agentic Operating Procedure

An engineering and operational blueprint for specifying, governing and assuring operational execution by agents that interpret context and act. The purpose is to make delegated operational execution inspectable: to state, before execution, who may decide what, under which constraints, and with what proof of outcome.

The estate at a glance

Fourteen repositories, one authority each, published under MIT. The interactive site is auto-synced from their main branches: schemas, fixtures, the exemplar corpus and the coverage map are pulled, not copied.

Certified Enforced

The reference harness passes 25 behavioural checks including poisoned-context authority injection and case-record tamper detection.

Adversarially tested

Must-reject fixtures: untrusted-grounded authority, republished unratified rules, append-only retraction, payload smuggling, proposer flooding.

Openly published

Every claim cites its artefact digest, profile version and cohort. Where evidence is missing, the coverage map says so.
