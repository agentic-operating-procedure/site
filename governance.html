Governance

Decision, change request, finding: three artefact classes, one decision authority, and a reviewer-independence layer that disciplines claims.

The three artefact classes

ADR

What was decided, and why. Immutable once accepted; superseding creates a new ADR.

Change request

A proposed change to the estate, the model, or a published artefact. Repository creation is a CR, not an edit.

Finding

What an investigation found, with the evidence. How the programme distinguishes verified from asserted.

Reviewer independence and claims discipline

Normative changes carry review from outside the authoring path. Two-approval quorum with recorded dissent that travels with dependent claims. Appeals through findings. Public claims cite version, profile, cohort, protocol, result and limitations. Invalidated claims are withdrawn in the place they were published.

Decision rights

Decision authority for structural change rests with the programme owner. Agents surface options with consequences; they do not decide. Every structural change is audited against the cascade checklist and lands through a change request with the cross-check recorded.

The full record

The complete CR register (CR-001 to CR-037) with landing states and named remainders is published in aop-governance, and the raw register file is synced into this site's data.
