The compile-and-run pipeline

Observe, model, compile, execute: a closed loop, not a relay. Execution evidence becomes new observation; the AOP stays true to the work.

Shadow mode: the bridge into execution

The compiled AOP first runs in observation-only mode against live cases: the guardrail decides what it would do, nothing executes, and every decision is compared with the choice the human actually made. Promotion to bounded execution is a decision made against the pre-registered agreement band, not against confidence.
